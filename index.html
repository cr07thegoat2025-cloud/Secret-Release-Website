TextChat

Texting, and nothing else.

TextChat is a messenger for Android that only does text. No calls, no ringing. Sign up with your email and start typing.

Download for Android

Free APK file. Android 6 or newer.

What you can do in TextChat

  • Sign up with emailPick a name, an email and a password. Forgot the password? Get a reset link by email.
  • Message in real timeMessages appear on your friend's phone as soon as you send them.
  • Find friends by emailType the email they signed up with and start a chat. Nobody can browse a list of users.
  • Keep chats privateOnly the two people in a conversation can read it.

Install it in three steps

  1. Download the fileTap the download button on this page from your Android phone. It saves a file called app-release.apk.
  2. Open itTap the finished download. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Create your accountOpen TextChat, tap Sign up, and confirm your email if the app asks you to.

Questions

Why does Android warn me about installing it?

TextChat isn't on the Play Store, so Android shows a warning for any app installed from a downloaded file. The app is built automatically on GitHub from public code, and you can read that code in the repository linked below.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. TextChat is only available for Android right now.

Can I make voice or video calls?

No. TextChat only sends text messages. That's the whole idea.

The download button says the app isn't ready

The first version hasn't been published yet. Check back soon, or open the releases page to see when it appears.
